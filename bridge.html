truss me

build a bridge, send the traffic over, try not to drop anyone in the river. pick a level.

3 stars2

1 2 3 materials, R reinforce, X delete, ctrl+Z undo, T test, esc let go. wheel zooms, right drag (or space drag) pans.

how to play: pick road, beam or cable, then tap a joint or an anchor (the yellow squares) and tap a grid dot to build a member there. you can also drag from a joint. vehicles only drive on road, and road can't span far on its own, so hold it up with triangles of beams. reinforce makes a member 1.5x as strong for 2x the cost, so save it for the red spots. hit test: members go green, yellow, red as they strain and snap at red. get every vehicle across to pass, spend less for more stars. two fingers pinch and pan on a phone.
