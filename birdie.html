the poo-o-meter is your fuel. it drains slowly all the time and pooing takes a chunk. eat to fill it back up. if it runs out, you lose a life.
the poo-cam (top left) looks straight down. arrows round its edge point to food: chips and pizza, cherries, carcass, fish, butterflies, twigs and your nest. not every bird eats everything.
nesting: pick up twigs (on the ground or in bins), land and press space to start a nest, bring more twigs, then press space in the finished nest to lay an egg. sit on it to keep it warm. when it hatches, feed the chick (space, next to it, when it's hungry) and it grows up and flies off, giving you a life.
houses: the windows are open. fly in, look round, and check the tables and counters for food. you can land on any roof, ledge, sill or bit of furniture.
lives: you have five. you lose one for crashing, landing too hard, power lines, cars, getting caught by people, falling in water (only seagulls and swans can swim) or running out of food.
