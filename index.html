How It Works

  1. pick a game. they all run right here in your browser.
  2. type a name when you finish to put your score on the board. no account needed.
  3. the daily boards wipe at midnight Singapore time. all-time boards stay forever.
  4. something off? hit feedback (or the pink Rate button and the stars on any game). we're in alpha, it all helps.
