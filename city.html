getting started: you land somewhere random with your fists and a bit of cash. find a building with a door and get inside: chests and gun cases indoors are where the real money and the big guns are. stuff on the pavement is small change.
zombies: there aren't many, and they're slow, but they don't give up easily once they've seen you. get too close and they come for you. you can outwalk them and easily outrun them. aim down the sights (right click) and go for the head: one shot does it.
E menu: your inventory (use, equip, place or sell anything), the shop (guns, ammo, armour, defences, cars, outfits) and the map. click anywhere on the map to teleport there, once a minute.
defences: barricades and steel walls block doorways, spike traps and landmines only go off under zombies (and wardens), turrets shoot zombies by themselves. everyone online sees what you build.
going up and down: office towers have a lift in the lobby up to a penthouse (strongboxes!) and the roof. smaller blocks have fire-escape ladders. green M kiosks on street corners go down to the metro: tunnels, dead trains and stations full of loot.
the wardens: a few are still about. they shoot zombies, but shoot one of them, take a police car or hijack someone in front of them and they'll come after you. keep out of sight and the stars fade.
