the shop
delivered instantly. vehicles you buy are parked next to you and stay in your garage (call them in again for free).
loot: gun cases (orange on the radar), chests and storage boxes (yellow) are dotted round the city: on pavements, in yards and parks, on beaches and on tables inside houses. walk up and press E. cases always have a gun. what's inside refills after 8 minutes.
money: cash from loot, wallets people drop, and valuables (watches, laptops, gold, diamonds) you sell in the shop. the shop button is in the bottom corner, or press B: guns, ammo, armour, medkits, outfits and vehicles, delivered right next to you. your stuff is saved in this browser.
vehicles: parked cars and motorbikes are yours for the taking (E). so is traffic, if you don't mind the driver being upset. somewhere in every industrial zone there's a hangar with a plane in it: taxi out onto the long road, full throttle, then pull up (down arrow).
the wardens: shoot, hijack or cause trouble where a warden can see and you get wanted stars. more stars, more wardens, then police cars. keep out of sight and the stars fade. get taken down and the hospital keeps 10% of your cash.
online: everyone (up to 50, people and birds) shares one city. names float over heads. you can shoot other players if you want to, or just drive around together.
as a bird: the poo-o-meter is your fuel, eat to fill it, poo on people for points, build a nest and raise a chick for an extra life. people with guns can shoot you down.
