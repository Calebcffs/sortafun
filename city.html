story mode: "Halcyon", twelve chapters over three nights, played on your own in the same city. you're the courier who delivered the part that switched on the broadcast. with Mari, Teo, Nana, Kofi and (eventually) Captain Varga, you've got until dawn after the third night to switch it off. checkpoints in every chapter, easy / normal / hard, and every chapter you finish pays into your online cash.
the goal: get out. everyone online shares one evacuation: fuel the radio mast at Broadcast Plaza (the red R on the map) with 12 fuel cans, then hold its console while the noise brings the horde, and a helicopter comes. be on the helipad when it leaves and you escape: $20,000 and a fresh start. then the mast needs fuel again.
day and night: the same clock for everyone. 11 minutes of golden hour, then dusk, then 5 and a half minutes of night: dark, twice as many zombies, runners that sprint, screamers that call the rest, brutes that shrug off headshots. get somewhere safe, keep your torch on (L) and stick together. survive to dawn and you get paid.
going down: at zero health you go down, not out. crawl, shoot, and hold on while someone holds F by you to pick you up, or use a medkit yourself. bleed out at night and you can rise as a zombie till dawn and hunt everyone else.
crews: the crew tab (E) invites players. crewmates are green, see your pings (Z) and quick chat (X) anywhere, and get more at dawn. buy a safehouse beacon: you heal and respawn by it, share a stash, and at night they raid it, so fortify it with barricades, mines and turrets.
getting stronger: XP for everything, levels, a perk every few levels (medic, engineer, deadeye...), and three jobs on the go in your inventory. supply drops parachute in every 4 minutes: crack one open (hold F) and everyone there gets a share.
loot and the city: the good stuff is indoors: houses, tower lobbies, penthouses (take the lift), the metro (green M kiosks). outdoors is small change. fuel drums are in the industrial yards.
