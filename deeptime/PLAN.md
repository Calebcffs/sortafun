Slender: The Eight Pages, beat for beat, but the thing in the woods came
through a hole in time. Found-footage analogue horror: you are watching a
recovered camcorder tape. First person, night, a flashlight, eight things to
find, and something that gets closer every time you find one.

## The backstory (told in the intro tape, the part tags and the ending)

**Hollow Creek Research Station, Garfield County, Montana. October 1987.**
Hell Creek country, where the real T. rex fossils come out of the ground.

Project CHRONOS was a Department of Energy program hidden inside a
paleontology dig. The idea: dig up a fossil, then use a machine (the
**Anchor**) to open a window back to the exact moment the animal was alive,
66 million years ago, and film it. Just a window. Look, don't touch.

At 23:14 on 14 October 1987 they ran the Anchor at full power. The window
opened wider than it was built to, and for about four seconds the forest at
Hollow Creek and the forest of the Late Cretaceous were the same place.
The Anchor blew apart. **Eight of its components were thrown across the
forest.** Things came through before the window closed. Two of them are
still here.

The Anchor still has one job left in it: the **recall**. With all eight
parts back in range of its core, it fires once and drags everything that
does not belong in 1987 back where it came from. Somebody has to walk into
the woods and bring the parts back.

You are **Dale Pruitt**, the night technician, the only one left on site.
You have a flashlight, the station's camcorder (it's taping everything,
that's the tape we're watching), and the fence.

**The two that stayed:**

- **The Watcher.** An adult dromaeosaur the station crew called "the tall
  one" (a Utahraptor, 5m nose to tail, taller than you at the hip). It came
  through half out of phase. It doesn't walk, it **skips**: one moment it's
  there, the next it's somewhere else, closer. The camcorder picks it up as
  static and tape damage. It is the Slender Man of this game.
- **The Queen.** A tyrannosaur. She is enormous, she is lost, and she is
  angry. Most of the time she is a sound: roars over the ridge, the ground
  thudding, the trees creaking. Later she walks through the forest. She
  hunts movement and light.

**The ending.** Part 8 arms the recall. The forest goes white. For one
second you are standing in the Cretaceous, and she is right there. Cut to
static. A text card: the tape was found at dawn in the Anchor crater. The
technician was not. One item of evidence: a tooth, 30cm, "unidentified".

**What's new (the prehistoric part):**

- **The Queen's rule.** From part 4 she walks through the woods. If she
  sees you moving fast or your light is on her, she stops and roars at you.
  You then have about 3 seconds: **stop moving and kill the light** and she
  loses you (sniffs, grunts, walks off). Keep running, keep the light on
  her, and she charges. The tags hint this ("DON'T RUN WHEN THE GROUND
  SHAKES").
- **The forest goes quiet.** The crickets and the owls stop when something
  big is near. That silence is the warning, before any footstep.
- **Deep time bleeds through.** The closer to the Anchor crater, the more
  the forest turns Cretaceous: giant ferns and cycads between the pines, warm
  wet air (fog turns faintly green), a distant insect drone that isn't from
  1987.
- **The pack.** From part 6, smaller raptors call to each other around you
  (clicks, barks, hisses, always directional, never seen clearly). They mean
  the Watcher is about to skip.

## The map (300m x 300m inside the fence)

The station gate (start) is on the south fence. Paths of packed dirt link
the landmarks; between them is dense pine with ferns and fallen trunks.

The ten landmarks (each has 3 possible spots for its part):

1. **Field office trailer**: the station trailer, door hanging open, a
   street lamp that flickers, a generator that won't start.
2. **The dig**: the excavation. Grid strings, tarps, a sifting screen, a
   half-dug skull in the rock.
3. **The truck**: the station pickup under a tarp, nosed into a tree, the
   jerrycans spilled.
4. **The culvert**: a concrete tunnel under the old access road berm.
   Pitch black, echoing.
5. **The water tower**: rusted, on four legs, a ladder you can't climb.
6. **The radio mast**: a lattice mast with a blinking red light, cable
   drums, utility boxes.
7. **The outhouses**: two plank outhouses and a trash pile. (For Slender.)
8. **The nest**: a ring of torn branches and turned earth, eggs, bones,
   the station's missing dog tag.
9. **The rocks**: a mossy outcrop with an overhang.
10. **The grove**: a huge dead tree in a ring of Cretaceous ferns, right on
    the edge of the crater. Time is thinnest here.

In the middle: **the Anchor crater**, scorched ground, the torn ring of the
machine, cables, a searchlight still on its side. Not a part spot. It's
where the recall fires.

Each is a real-looking object (Poly Haven CC0 models) with a hazard tag.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chronometer | vintage pocket watch | IT DOESNT WALK. IT SKIPS. |
| 2 | Phase instrument | vintage spacecraft instrument | THE CRICKETS STOP FIRST |
| 3 | Carrier transceiver | vintage radio transceiver | DONT RUN WHEN THE GROUND SHAKES |
| 4 | Calibration tape | portable cassette player | SHE CANT SEE YOU IF YOU DONT MOVE |
| 5 | Focus lens | vintage microscope | DONT LOOK AT THE TALL ONE |

| 8 | Field kit | metal toolbox | IT KNOWS WHAT YOU CARRY |

Order of notes is fixed (1st pickup shows note 1) whatever object it is.

## Look: photoreal, dark, recorded on tape

- three.js r160, PBR everywhere: Poly Haven CC0 photoscanned ground,
  bark, rock, metal and wood; ACES tone mapping; the flashlight is the main
  light (spot with a lens cookie and shadows, so trunks throw moving
  shadows). Moonlight is barely there. Black exponential fog.
- Trees: instanced trunks with bark PBR (a few hundred to a couple of
  thousand), canopy mostly unseen overhead. Ground cover: ferns, shrubs,
  dead branches, stumps, roots, mossy rocks (Poly Haven models).
- Dinosaurs: Quaternius CC0 T. rex and Velociraptor rigs, subdivided twice
  in Blender for smooth silhouettes, skinned with a procedural reptile
  shader (scales in the normal map, mottled albedo, wet speculars) and
  eyeshine that catches the flashlight.
- The tape: film grain, chromatic aberration, scanlines, head-switching
  noise along the bottom, tracking wobble, static bursts, a slight vignette
  and softness. The camcorder OSD: `PLAY >`, `PARTS n/8`, `BATT`, and
  `OCT 14 1987 11:47PM` counting up.

## Sound: most of the fear

WebAudio, HRTF panning on everything in the world, a generated forest
reverb, lowpass with distance. All sounds are CC0 from Freesound
(previews), credited in `deeptime/CREDITS.md`.

- **Ambience:** crickets, night wind in the pines, a creaking tree, an owl,
  far thunder. Crickets duck out when a dinosaur is near.
- **You:** footsteps sliced from real recordings (leaves, dirt path,
  gravel, wood, concrete in the culvert), faster and heavier when running;
  breathing that follows stamina and fear; a heartbeat that follows the
  Watcher; flashlight click.
- **The forest lying to you:** twig snaps and branch cracks behind you,
  bushes rustling, placed in 3D.
- **The Queen:** roars (distant ones with long reverb), snorts, huge
  footfalls with camera shake and a sub rumble.
- **The Watcher and the pack:** hisses, snarls, the raptor shriek, clicks,
  breathing that you only hear when it's close.
- **The tape:** hiss bed, static when the Watcher is in view, a stinger on
  each skip you see, the jumpscare.
- **Escalation music:** synthesised layers, as in Slender.

## Controls

WASD move, mouse look (click to lock), Shift run, **F** or right click the
flashlight, **E** or left click pick up, scroll wheel camcorder zoom, Esc
pause. Phones: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, buttons for RUN, LIGHT,
GRAB.

## Score

Leaderboard `deeptime`, higher is better: `parts * 10000 - seconds` (so 8
parts in 12:30 beats 8 parts in 15:00, and any 8 beats any 7). Shown as
"8/8 in 12:30" or "5/8".
